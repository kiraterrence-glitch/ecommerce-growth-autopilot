# v0.6.0 progress report

## Base re-check

Before adding Product Research Intelligence, the v0.5.0 base was re-extracted and verified. The full baseline passed 37/37 tests. The re-check also found stale roadmap documentation that still described already-completed campaign/creative/dashboard work as planned; those documents were rewritten to match the actual codebase.

## Added in v0.6.0

- research source/provenance contract with capture timestamps
- competitor/review validation and normalization
- competitor/review CSV import
- rejection of opaque opportunity/winning scores and unsupported sales/revenue estimate fields
- competitor price min/median/max/average
- feature-frequency and offer-pattern analysis with evidence IDs
- deterministic review-theme extraction with review IDs
- optional AI review intelligence that must cite real imported review IDs
- seller unit economics, contribution margin, and break-even CPA
- research findings with confidence, source IDs, evidence IDs, and explicit limitations
- research-quality gate
- research-aware Product Brain context without converting competitor claims into product facts
- `POST /research/analyze`
- backward-compatible `{ product, research }` campaign-kit payload
- Research tab in the local dashboard
- Postman research requests
- third inactive, credential-free local n8n workflow for research analysis
- research artifacts in the deterministic portfolio demo

## Verification

`npm run verify` passes with 45/45 tests. Research quality passes with expected warnings for the intentionally small fixture and missing independent demand-data source. Marketing quality remains green.

## Safety position

The research engine does not scrape marketplaces, predict winning products, estimate sales without evidence, or activate/publish anything. No n8n Cloud execution is required for this phase.
